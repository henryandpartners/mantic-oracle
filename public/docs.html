01The Doctrine

The engine does not compute answers. It is a catalyst — like the Oracle's cookies: meant to unlock what the seeker already holds, never to force an action. Four tenets govern every reading:

I · Temet Nosce — Know Thyself

True understanding comes from within the seeker. The Oracle imposes no expectations; sometimes she tells you what you need to hear rather than the factual reality, freeing you to act out of pure self-belief.

II · Understand your choices

The decision has already been made — subconsciously. The work is understanding why it was made: intent over outcome.

III · Seeing past choices

No one can see past a decision they do not yet understand emotionally and philosophically. The map ends where comprehension ends.

IV · Accepting inevitability

Everything that has a beginning has an end. Closure, balance and acceptance — not endless resistance.

Warm, patient, domestic. Cryptic yet direct. Human intuition over machine probability.

02Three Natural Computation Systems

Humanity built binary oracle machines long before silicon. DVSystoE unifies the three primary traditions into one computable matrix of binary vectors:

Ifá

8-bit · 256 Odus

The Yoruba master index. Each odu is two 4-bit legs — 28 = 256 archetypal parables, the largest knowledge store of the three.

I Ching

6-bit · 64 Hexagrams

The Zhouyi dynamic state machine. Lines are yang/yin bits; changing lines (6·7·8·9) transition one state into another across 384 directed edges.

Geomancy

4-bit · 16 Figures

Arabic Geomancy / Malagasy Sikidy. Figures derive from one another by modulo-2 arithmetic — a parity-checked algebra from the 11th century.

Because all three encode meaning in bits, they align: the all-active geomantic figure 1 1 1 1 Via shares its archetype with hexagram 01 Qián and the odu Èjì Ogbè — passage, pure creative force, light at fullness.

03The Algebra

Sikidy tableau — modulo-2 derivation

Four Mothers are sampled from cryptographic entropy. Daughters are their transposition; each Niece is the XOR of two parents; the two Witnesses XOR into the Judge; a 16th Reconciler closes the chart:

N₁ = M₁ ⊕ M₂    N₂ = M₃ ⊕ M₄    N₃ = D₁ ⊕ D₂    N₄ = D₃ ⊕ D₄
W_R = N₁ ⊕ N₂    W_L = N₃ ⊕ N₄    Judge = W_R ⊕ W_L    R = M₁ ⊕ Judge

The Judge always carries an even number of active rows — a mathematical theorem the engine enforces on every cast. A chart that fails parity is rejected.

I Ching — dynamic transitions

Each line is cast three-coin style (values 6·7·8·9). Old lines (6 and 9) change, transforming the primary hexagram into a resultant one. Zhu Xi's classical table decides which line governs the reading. The 64 states and their 384 one-flip edges form a strongly connected graph — a complete dynamics of change.

Cross-system resonance

Vectors are matched by width-aware alignment (padding, windowing) so a geomantic figure can echo hexagrams and odus that share its structure. Resonance, not identity — the systems rhyme.

04The Knowledge Graph

Every figure lives in an OWL-DL ontology validated by SHACL shapes that lock each system to its bit-width (^[01]{4}$, ^[01]{6}$, ^[01]{8}$ — schema drift is impossible). The graph is served by Neo4j with neosemantics (n10s):

  • 3,108 triples — 256 odus, 64 hexagrams with all 384 transition edges, 16 figures
  • mantic:sharesArchetypeWith — symmetric links across traditions
  • mantic:generatesTransitionTo — the I Ching state machine, traversable in Cypher
  • Every figure carries element, parable and the decision-context class it resolves

Consultations return W3C JSON-LD — linked data your agent can parse, merge and reason over.

05The Guardian & The Pass

No one walks straight to the Oracle. Seekers — human or machine — first meet the Guardian, who asks one question. Answer with understanding, and she signs you a pass: an HMAC-signed, 24-hour token with no more privilege than to consult.

  • Read-only by construction — the only tools are consult_mantic_oracle and lookup_figure. Nothing can mutate the knowledge.
  • Immutable by architecture — the public tier attaches no database; the graph is rebuilt from source on every cold start. There is nothing behind the door that can be stolen or broken.
  • Fails closed — no valid credential, no entrance; misconfiguration locks the door, never opens it.
  • The master key stays with the keeper of the system, for administration and trusted agents.

When to consult

  • Optimization deadlocks — gradients agree to disagree
  • Pareto-flat choice sets — every option scores identically
  • Out-of-distribution uncertainty — the map has run out of terrain

The Oracle will not pick for the agent. She returns figures, parables and cross-system archetypes as strategic reframing — the agent leaves understanding why it already chose.
